## How to get support

| Tier | Channel |
|---|---|

| Professional | Email [support@lpdf.io](mailto:support@lpdf.io) from the address on your portal account |

Licensing, billing and account questions go to [license@lpdf.io](mailto:license@lpdf.io) at any tier,
including Community.

**Security issues.** Email [support@lpdf.io](mailto:support@lpdf.io) with `SECURITY` at the start of
the subject line, at any tier including Community. Please do not open a public issue for a suspected
vulnerability. We aim to acknowledge security reports within one business day.

## Response times

Response times are **targets for a first substantive response**, not resolution times. A response
means a human has read your report, reproduced it or asked for what is needed to reproduce it, and
told you what happens next.

Business hours are **09:00–17:00 US Central Time, Monday to Friday**, excluding United States public
holidays. All targets below are measured in business hours or business days on that clock.

| Severity | Meaning | Community | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — Blocked** | Document generation fails in a production system and no workaround is available | Best effort | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| **S2 — Impaired** | Incorrect output or degraded behaviour, with a workaround available | Best effort | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| **S3 — Question** | Usage, integration, schema or documentation questions | Best effort | 3 business days | 1 business day |

We set the severity on receipt, and will tell you if we assess it differently from you. If you
disagree, say so and we will discuss it — severity is about impact on your production system, not
about urgency of feeling.

**Community support carries no commitment.** It is provided by us and by other users on a best-effort
basis, and nothing on this page creates an obligation at that tier. In practice most questions get
answered; that is a description of how we work, not a promise.

## What support covers

**In scope:**

- Defects in Lpdf — output that does not match the documented behaviour of the schema or SDK
- Questions about documented behaviour, the XML schema, data binding, design tokens and SDK
  integration
- Guidance on structuring a document to achieve a particular layout
- License key, portal, renewal and provisioning problems
- Regressions introduced by an Lpdf release

**Out of scope:**

- Debugging your application, your data, or code that does not call Lpdf
- Writing your documents or templates for you
- Modified copies of Lpdf, or builds we did not publish
- Problems caused by third-party libraries, runtimes or infrastructure
- Unregistered use — evaluation without a license key is unsupported, though we will always answer a
  question about how to obtain one

Work that falls outside this scope — template development, integration work, custom features — is
available separately. Ask at [license@lpdf.io](mailto:license@lpdf.io).

## What we need from you

The single largest factor in how quickly something is resolved is whether we can reproduce it. A
report that includes the following is usually resolved in one exchange rather than four:

- Lpdf version, SDK and language runtime version, operating system
- The smallest document or code sample that reproduces the problem
- What you expected, and what you got — attach the PDF if the output is wrong
- Any error message in full, including the stack trace

Please do not send us production data. A reduced sample with representative structure is more useful
and avoids putting your data anywhere it does not belong.

## What this page does not promise

**There is no availability or uptime commitment, at any tier.** Lpdf runs entirely inside your own
infrastructure — it makes no network calls and depends on no service we operate. There is nothing for
us to keep running, so an uptime SLA would be meaningless. The portal at
[my.codesense.dev](https://my.codesense.dev) is used to issue and renew license keys, not to generate
documents, so a portal outage does not stop your documents rendering.

**We do not commit to fixing every reported defect, or to a fix within a stated time.** We commit to
responding within the times above, to telling you honestly whether and when we expect to fix
something, and to giving you a workaround where one exists.

**Response times apply while your license is current.** Support ends when a subscription lapses, in
line with the [Lpdf License](/license).
